# APEX AFRICA

### Engineering the Future of African Motorsport
This is an interactive motorsport simulation and engineering project inspired by Rwanda's vision of bringing Formula 1 back to Africa. We've gotta be excited for this

APEX AFRICA explores the intersection of racing, technology, and innovation through simulation, artificial intelligence, and engineering. SIGH
